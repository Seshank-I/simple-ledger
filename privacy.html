₹ Simple Ledger — Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

In short: your records stay on your phone. The developer never receives any of your data. The only copies that leave the phone are ones you choose to make.

What the app stores

The names and optional phone numbers of people you add, the money entries you record (amounts, dates, notes), interest rates, and app settings. All of this is stored only on your phone.

When data leaves your phone

  • Google Drive backup (optional, off until you turn it on). If you connect Google Drive, the app uploads a backup file of your records to a folder named “Simple Ledger Backups” in your own Google Drive. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it created itself, never anything else in your Drive. You can turn this off in the app’s Settings and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Backups and statements you share. When you tap “Send backup” or “Send statement”, the file goes only to the app or person you choose (for example WhatsApp or email).
  • Voice input (optional). When you tap “Speak”, your phone’s own speech recognition service (for example Google or Samsung) turns your voice into text, under that provider’s privacy policy. On phones that support it, the text is then understood by Gemini Nano running on the phone itself; nothing is sent anywhere for that step.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up with the developer.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • The developer has no server and cannot see your records or your Drive backups.

Google user data

Simple Ledger’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive access is used only to store and manage your own backup files.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes the records on the phone. Drive backups stay in your Drive until you delete the “Simple Ledger Backups” folder.
