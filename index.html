₹ Simple Ledger

Simple Ledger is a personal Android app for keeping track of money you lend to and borrow from family and friends. It is made for older users: large text, big buttons and plain sentences such as “Ramesh owes you ₹500”.

What it does

  • Records money given and received, with an optional note and date.
  • Keeps every entry forever. A mistake is fixed by adding a cancelling entry, never by changing or deleting history.
  • Optional simple interest per person.
  • Sends a statement picture on WhatsApp.
  • Voice entry in English and Telugu.
  • Optional daily backup to your own Google Drive.

The app has no account, no server and no advertising. It is not on the Play Store; it is shared privately with family.
